# Big Exec Product Design Principles

These are acceptance requirements, not optional polish.

1. **One connected product.** Every authenticated page uses the same Big Exec product shell, navigation language, hierarchy, component language, spacing rhythm, responsive rules, and black/gold brand foundation. Page-specific atmosphere may vary without making the page feel like a different application.
2. **Beginner-first, expert-capable.** A first-time fantasy player must understand the next action without knowing players, teams, roster terminology, or strategy. Experienced players must be able to reach deeper statistics and faster controls without slowing the beginner path.
3. **Guide roster construction.** Draft and lineup experiences explain required positions, support position-first browsing, allow direct search, expose availability, prevent accidental actions, and never require scrolling a complete player database to perform a basic task.
4. **Best product, transparent cost.** Zero current users is a cost-planning fact, not a reduced-quality target. Proposals should identify the strongest experience, implementation options, recurring costs, and scaling thresholds.
5. **Original visual identity.** Use Big Exec marks and generated franchise crests only. Do not use professional-league branding, professional-team logos, uniforms, player photographs, or recognizable player likenesses. Player names, factual statistics, game status, and results are informational data subject to provider terms and legal review.
6. **Franchise ownership matters.** Franchise name, abbreviation, colors, crest, stadium, achievements, rivalries, and history must feel persistent and connected throughout the product.
7. **Accessible by default.** Keyboard access, visible focus, semantic labels, screen-reader status, reduced motion, readable contrast, and mobile touch targets are release requirements.
8. **Prove critical promises.** Realtime drafting, timers, autopick, scoring, and recap rendering remain unproven until verified end to end in production-like conditions. Gate 5 does not pass on screenshots or builds alone.
