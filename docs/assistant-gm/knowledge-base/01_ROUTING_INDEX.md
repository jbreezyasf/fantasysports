## Router rules

- Multiple intents: answer the urgent transactional/state question first, then the educational question.
- Ambiguous player or league: ask a short clarifying question before retrieving or acting.
- “Why” questions about a specific result: retrieve the exact event/rule/state; do not answer from generic FAQ alone.
- Current injuries, kickoff, weather, projections, player availability, and deadlines are dynamic and require current approved data.
- Payment and entitlement questions may be explained, but the Assistant GM has no payment tool and cannot change billing.
