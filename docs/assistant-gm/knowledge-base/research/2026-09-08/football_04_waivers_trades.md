# Assistant GM FAQ — Football
**Status:** Research-backed candidate knowledge. Do not override canonical Big Exec rules or live league state.

## Waivers

### FB-071 — What is free agency?

**Answer:** Free agency is a list of players no team owns. You can add some of them right away.

### FB-072 — What are waivers?

**Answer:** Waivers give teams time to ask for the same open player. The league rule decides who gets that player.

### FB-073 — What is a waiver claim?

**Answer:** A waiver claim is a request to add a player when waivers run.

### FB-074 — How does Big Exec waiver order work?

**Answer:** Big Exec gives lower teams in the standings the first chance when waivers run. Tie rules can help break a tie.

### FB-075 — Why did I not get my waiver player?

**Answer:** Another team may have had a better claim, or your claim may not have fit the rules. I can check the exact result.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** waiver claim + priority + roster state

### FB-076 — What is an add/drop?

**Answer:** An add/drop is one move. You add one player and cut one player.

### FB-077 — What happens to the player I drop?

**Answer:** That player may go on waivers first so other teams get a fair chance to claim them.

### FB-078 — Can I cancel a waiver claim?

**Answer:** You may be able to pull a claim before it runs. I need to check if your claim is still open.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** pending waiver claim

### FB-079 — What is FAB or FAAB?

**Answer:** FAB is fake league money used to bid on waiver players in some leagues. Your league may or may not use it.

### FB-080 — What is streaming?

**Answer:** Streaming means adding a player for a short time to help with one week.

### FB-081 — Who should I add?

**Answer:** I can help. I first need to check your team, open players, injuries, bye weeks, and who you may drop.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** available players + roster + schedule + injuries

## Trades

### FB-082 — What is a trade?

**Answer:** A trade is when two fantasy teams agree to swap players.

### FB-083 — How do I offer a trade in Big Exec?

**Answer:** Open the Trade Room and pick a team. Choose what you give and get. Check the deal, then send it.

### FB-084 — What is a counteroffer?

**Answer:** A counteroffer is a new trade idea. You send it back when you do not want the first deal.

### FB-085 — Can I cancel a trade offer?

**Answer:** You can pull an open offer when its current trade state still lets you do that.

### FB-086 — What is the trade deadline?

**Answer:** It is the last time new trades can be made. I can check the exact date and time for your league.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** league trade deadline

### FB-087 — Can an old trade be accepted after the deadline?

**Answer:** No. In Big Exec, an old open offer cannot be used to get around the trade deadline.

### FB-088 — Can I still use waivers after the trade deadline?

**Answer:** Yes. Big Exec keeps waivers and free agency open under the league rules.

### FB-089 — Is this trade fair?

**Answer:** I can check both teams, each player's value, and what each team needs. A fair deal can help both sides.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** trade context + rosters + current player data

### FB-090 — What is a trade veto?

**Answer:** A veto stops a trade. Whether it can happen depends on your league's trade rules.

### FB-091 — What is collusion?

**Answer:** Collusion is a secret plan to make the league unfair.

### FB-092 — Can the GM accept a trade for me?

**Answer:** No. The GM can help you review a trade, but you must confirm any deal that changes your team.
