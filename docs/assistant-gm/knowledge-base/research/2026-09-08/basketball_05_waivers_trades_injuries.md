## Waivers

### BB-101 — What is free agency in fantasy basketball?

**Answer:** Free agency is a list of players no team owns. You can add some of them right away.

### BB-102 — What are waivers?

**Answer:** Waivers give teams time to ask for the same open player. The league rule decides who gets that player.

### BB-103 — What is a waiver claim?

**Answer:** A waiver claim is a request to add a player when waivers run.

### BB-104 — What is waiver priority?

**Answer:** Waiver priority is the order for claims. It helps pick a winner when two teams want the same player.

### BB-105 — What is FAB or FAAB?

**Answer:** FAB is fake league money used to bid on waiver players in some leagues.

### BB-106 — Why did my waiver not go through?

**Answer:** Someone else may have won, your bid may have lost, or the move may have broken a roster rule. I can check.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** waiver result + roster rules

### BB-107 — What is an add/drop?

**Answer:** An add/drop is one move. You add one player and cut one player.

### BB-108 — Do I get stats a player scored before I added them?

**Answer:** No. Stats from games before the player joined your team do not get added to your team.

### BB-109 — Who should I add?

**Answer:** I can help. I first need to check your rules, open players, games, injuries, and team needs.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

### BB-110 — Should I drop a cold player?

**Answer:** Not just because of a few bad games. Check role, minutes, health, and your team needs before you cut them.

## Trades

### BB-111 — What is a fantasy basketball trade?

**Answer:** A trade is when two fantasy teams agree to swap players.

### BB-112 — What is a two-for-one trade?

**Answer:** One team gives two players and gets one player back. The open roster spot can also have value.

### BB-113 — What is a counteroffer?

**Answer:** A counteroffer is a new trade idea. You send it back when you do not want the first deal.

### BB-114 — Is this trade fair?

## Injuries

### BB-118 — What does questionable mean in basketball?

**Answer:** Questionable means we do not know for sure if the player will play. Check fresh news near game time.

### BB-119 — What does out mean?

**Answer:** Out means the player will not play in that game.

### BB-120 — What does game-time decision mean?

**Answer:** It means the final word on the player's status may come close to game time.

### BB-121 — What is a minutes limit?

**Answer:** A minutes limit means a player will play less than normal. This can happen after a hurt player comes back.

### BB-122 — Why does a minutes limit matter?

**Answer:** Less time on the court can mean fewer chances to add stats.

### BB-123 — Why do players rest on back-to-backs?

**Answer:** Some teams rest players to help manage health and work load. This can change with little notice.

### BB-124 — What happens if a real game is postponed?

**Answer:** The game moves to a new time. Whether the old lineup spot still counts depends on your fantasy league rules.

### BB-125 — What should I do with an injured player?

**Answer:** Check the injury tag and return news. Also check your IL spots and open players before you choose.

### BB-126 — What if my player becomes healthy while in IL?

**Answer:** Your league may make you move the player out of IL before you make some other moves.
