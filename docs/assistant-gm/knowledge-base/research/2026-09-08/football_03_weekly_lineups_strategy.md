# Assistant GM FAQ — Football
**Status:** Research-backed candidate knowledge. Do not override canonical Big Exec rules or live league state.

## Weekly play

### FB-054 — What should I do each week?

**Answer:** Check your starters, injuries, bye weeks, waivers, and trades. Make sure each starter is ready before game time.

### FB-055 — When does my player lock?

**Answer:** In Big Exec, a player locks when that player's real game starts.

### FB-056 — Does my whole lineup lock when the first game starts?

**Answer:** No. In Big Exec, each player locks when that player's own game starts.

### FB-057 — Can I move a Sunday player after a Thursday game starts?

**Answer:** Yes, if the Sunday player has not started. The move must still make a legal lineup.

### FB-058 — Do bench points count?

**Answer:** No. Bench points do not count in a normal Big Exec weekly lineup.

### FB-059 — Who should I start?

**Answer:** I can help. I first need to check your players, injuries, game times, and league rules.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** roster + lineup + injury + schedule + projections

### FB-060 — What should I do with a player on a bye?

**Answer:** Move the bye player out of your starting group. Start someone who plays that week.

### FB-061 — What should I do if my starter is out?

**Answer:** Move the hurt player to your bench or IR if you can. Then start a player who can play.

### FB-062 — Why can't I move a player?

**Answer:** The player may be locked, the spot may not fit, or the move may make your lineup wrong. I can check.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** lineup lock + position fit + roster rules

### FB-063 — Why put a later game player in FLEX?

**Answer:** It can give you more swap choices if late news comes in. The player still must fit the FLEX spot.

### FB-064 — Does weather matter?

**Answer:** Sometimes. Strong wind, rain, snow, or heat can change a game plan. I should check fresh weather before I use it.

**Answer type:** Dynamic — check live state before answering.

**Internal tool note:** current weather + game context

### FB-065 — What is a floor?

**Answer:** A floor is a rough idea of a player's safer low score. It is still only a guess.

### FB-066 — What is upside?

**Answer:** Upside means a player has a chance to score much more than usual.

### FB-067 — What is a stack?

**Answer:** A stack means two starters can score on the same play. A QB and one of his pass catchers is one case.

### FB-068 — What is a safe play?

**Answer:** A safe play is a choice with a steadier role and fewer big risks. It may have less upside.

### FB-069 — What is a boom-or-bust player?

**Answer:** This player may score a lot or very little. Their weekly score can swing more than most.

### FB-070 — What is a game-time call?

**Answer:** It means the final word on a player may come close to game time.

## Strategy

### FB-119 — What is a waiver wire?

**Answer:** The waiver wire is a list of open players. You may need to make a claim before you can add one.

### FB-120 — What is roster depth?

**Answer:** Roster depth means you have good backup players, not just good starters.
