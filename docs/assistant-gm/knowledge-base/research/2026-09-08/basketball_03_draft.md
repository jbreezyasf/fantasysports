## Draft

### BB-051 — What is a fantasy basketball draft?

**Answer:** The draft is when each team picks its players. Teams take turns making picks.

### BB-052 — What is a snake draft?

**Answer:** A snake draft flips the pick order each round. Early picks in one round become later picks in the next.

### BB-053 — What is draft order?

**Answer:** Draft order tells which manager picks first, second, and so on.

### BB-054 — What does on the clock mean?

**Answer:** On the clock means it is your turn to make a draft pick.

### BB-055 — What is a draft queue?

**Answer:** A draft queue is your private list of players you want to pick.

### BB-056 — What is an auto pick?

**Answer:** An auto pick is a pick the game makes for you when needed. The site uses its draft rules to choose.

### BB-057 — What does available mean in the draft?

**Answer:** Available means no team in your league has picked that player yet.

### BB-058 — What is ADP?

**Answer:** ADP means average draft spot. It shows about where a player is being picked in many drafts.

### BB-059 — What is a player rank?

**Answer:** A rank puts players in an order. It helps you compare them in the draft.

### BB-060 — What is a reach?

**Answer:** A reach is a player picked much earlier than most drafts expect.

### BB-061 — What is a sleeper?

**Answer:** A sleeper may do far better than most people think.

### BB-063 — Should I draft for points or positions?

**Answer:** You need both. Take good players, but make sure you can fill the lineup spots your league uses.

### BB-064 — Should I draft the same way in points and category leagues?

**Answer:** No. A player's value can change a lot when the scoring rules change.

### BB-065 — Should I draft only big scorers?

**Answer:** Not always. Rebounds, assists, steals, blocks, shooting, and other stats may matter in your league.

### BB-066 — Do real NBA positions matter in fantasy?

**Answer:** Yes. Your lineup has spots, and a player must be allowed to fit the spot you want to use.

### BB-067 — What is position depth?

**Answer:** Position depth means how many good players are still left at one spot.

### BB-068 — What is a balanced category team?

**Answer:** It is a team that can help in many stat groups. It does not give up too much in many areas.

### BB-069 — Should I draft hurt players?

**Answer:** It depends on the injury, your bench, your IL spots, and when the player may return. I should check fresh news.

### BB-070 — How much should I trust preseason ranks?

**Answer:** Use ranks as a guide, not a promise. Your league rules and team build still matter.
