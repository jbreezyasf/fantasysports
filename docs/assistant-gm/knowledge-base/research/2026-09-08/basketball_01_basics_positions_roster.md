# Assistant GM FAQ — Basketball
**Status:** Research-backed candidate knowledge. Do not override canonical Big Exec rules or live league state.
**Basketball boundary:** Big Exec basketball rules are not yet canonical. Treat these as common fantasy-basketball education unless future product rules or live league settings confirm them.

## Basics

### BB-001 — What is fantasy basketball?

**Answer:** You draft real basketball players for your fantasy team. What they do in real games helps your fantasy team.

### BB-002 — Is fantasy basketball played every day?

**Answer:** Many leagues let you set players each day. Some leagues use weekly lineups or other weekly game rules.

### BB-003 — What is a roster?

**Answer:** Your roster is the full list of players your team owns.

### BB-004 — What is a starting lineup?

**Answer:** Your starting lineup is the group of players whose game stats can count for your team.

### BB-005 — What is the bench?

**Answer:** The bench holds players you own but are not starting. Their stats do not count while they are benched.

## Positions

### BB-006 — What is a PG?

**Answer:** PG means point guard. This player often handles the ball and sets up the offense.

### BB-007 — What is an SG?

**Answer:** SG means shooting guard. This player often shoots, scores, and helps move the ball.

### BB-008 — What is an SF?

**Answer:** SF means small forward. This player may score, rebound, pass, and guard many kinds of players.

### BB-009 — What is a PF?

**Answer:** PF means power forward. This player often rebounds, scores near the rim, and may shoot from outside.

### BB-010 — What is a C?

**Answer:** C means center. This player often rebounds, blocks shots, and scores near the rim.

### BB-011 — What is a G spot?

**Answer:** G means guard. A player who can play point guard or shooting guard may fit there.

### BB-012 — What is an F spot?

**Answer:** F means forward. A player who can play small forward or power forward may fit there.

### BB-013 — What is UTIL?

**Answer:** UTIL is a free starting spot. Any player allowed by your league can usually fit there.

### BB-014 — What does position fit mean?

**Answer:** It means a player is allowed to go into that lineup spot.

### BB-015 — Can one player have more than one position?

**Answer:** Yes. Some players can fit more than one spot. Their listed spots can change by platform or season.

## Roster

### BB-016 — What is IL?

**Answer:** IL is an injured list spot. It can hold some hurt players when their injury tag fits the league rule.

### BB-017 — What is IL+?

**Answer:** IL+ is a wider injury spot used on some sites. It may allow more injury tags than a normal IL spot.

### BB-018 — What does active mean?

**Answer:** Active means the player is in a lineup spot where their game can count.

### BB-019 — What does DNP mean?

**Answer:** DNP means did not play. If the player does not enter the real game, they may give you no stats.

### BB-020 — What does rest mean?

**Answer:** Rest means the real team chose not to play the player. The player may not be hurt.
