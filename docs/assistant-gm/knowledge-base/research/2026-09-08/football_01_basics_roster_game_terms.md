# Assistant GM FAQ — Football
**Status:** Research-backed candidate knowledge. Do not override canonical Big Exec rules or live league state.

## Basics

### FB-001 — What is fantasy football?

**Answer:** You draft real football players for your fantasy team. Their real game stats turn into fantasy points.

### FB-002 — What is a roster?

**Answer:** Your roster is the full list of players your team owns.

### FB-003 — What is a starting lineup?

**Answer:** Your starting lineup is the group of players whose points count for your game that week.

### FB-004 — What is the bench?

**Answer:** The bench holds players you own but are not starting. Their points do not count that week.

## Positions

### FB-005 — What is a QB?

**Answer:** QB means quarterback. This player throws passes and may also run with the ball.

### FB-006 — What is an RB?

**Answer:** RB means running back. This player often runs the ball and may catch passes too.

### FB-007 — What is a WR?

**Answer:** WR means wide receiver. A WR catches passes.

### FB-008 — What is a TE?

**Answer:** TE means tight end. This player can block, catch passes, and score touchdowns.

### FB-009 — What is FLEX?

**Answer:** FLEX is a spot that gives you choices. In Big Exec football, you can start an RB, WR, or TE there.

### FB-010 — What is a K?

**Answer:** K means kicker. Kickers can score fantasy points from field goals and extra points.

### FB-011 — What is D/ST?

**Answer:** D/ST means defense and special teams. That unit can score from sacks, takeaways, stops, and touchdowns.

## Roster

### FB-012 — What is IR?

**Answer:** IR is a spot for some hurt players. A player must have the right injury tag to use it.

### FB-013 — How many starters are in Big Exec football?

**Answer:** The current setup has one QB, two RBs, two WRs, one TE, one FLEX, one K, and one D/ST.

### FB-014 — How many bench spots are in Big Exec football?

**Answer:** The current Big Exec setup has six bench spots.

### FB-015 — How many IR spots are in Big Exec football?

**Answer:** The current Big Exec setup has one IR spot.

## Game terms

### FB-016 — What is a bye week?

**Answer:** A bye is a week when a real football team does not play. A player on bye will not score that week.

### FB-017 — What does questionable mean?

**Answer:** Questionable means we do not know for sure if the player will play. Check again close to game time.

### FB-018 — What does out mean?

**Answer:** Out means the player will not play in that game.

### FB-019 — What does inactive mean?

**Answer:** Inactive means the player is not allowed to play in that game.

### FB-020 — What is a projection?

**Answer:** A projection is a guess at how many fantasy points a player may score. It is not a promise.

### FB-021 — What is a matchup?

**Answer:** A matchup is one game in your fantasy week. Your team plays one other team.

### FB-022 — What is head-to-head play?

**Answer:** Head-to-head means you face one team at a time. The team with the better score wins that matchup.

### FB-023 — What are Points For?

**Answer:** Points For is the total fantasy points your team has scored in games that count.

### FB-024 — What is a player target?

**Answer:** A target is a pass thrown toward a player. More targets can mean more chances to catch the ball.

### FB-025 — What is a touch?

**Answer:** A touch means a player carried the ball or caught a pass.

### FB-026 — What is the red zone?

**Answer:** The red zone is the last 20 yards before the end zone. Plays there can lead to more touchdown chances.

### FB-027 — What is a snap?

**Answer:** A snap is one play that starts when the center gives the ball to the quarterback.

### FB-028 — What is a depth chart?

**Answer:** A depth chart shows who is first, second, or next at each spot on a real team.

### FB-029 — What is a backup?

**Answer:** A backup is a player behind the main starter. The backup may play more if the starter gets hurt.
