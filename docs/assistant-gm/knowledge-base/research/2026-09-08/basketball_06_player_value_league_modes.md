## Player value

### BB-127 — What does usage mean?

**Answer:** Usage shows how often a player ends a play with a shot, free throw, or lost ball.

### BB-128 — Why do minutes matter?

**Answer:** More minutes give a player more time to add stats. That can mean more fantasy help.

### BB-129 — Does starting in the NBA mean a player is always better for fantasy?

**Answer:** No. Some bench players play many minutes and have a big role. Role and stats matter more than the label.

### BB-130 — What is a hot streak?

**Answer:** A hot streak is a short run of strong games. It may keep going, or it may end soon.

### BB-131 — What is a cold streak?

**Answer:** A cold streak is a short run of poor games. It does not always mean the player's true value changed.

### BB-132 — What is a season-long hold?

**Answer:** A hold is a player you keep because you think they can help for much of the year.

### BB-133 — What is a short-term add?

**Answer:** A short-term add is a player you keep for a few games or days to help now.

### BB-136 — Should I chase a player after one huge game?

**Answer:** Usually not from one game alone. Check minutes, role, health, and whether the change looks real.

### BB-137 — What is a rookie?

**Answer:** A rookie is a player in their first year in the NBA.

### BB-138 — Are rookies risky in fantasy basketball?

**Answer:** They can be. Their minutes and role may change more as the season goes on.

## League types

### BB-139 — What is redraft?

**Answer:** Redraft means you make a new team each fantasy year.

### BB-140 — What is keeper?

**Answer:** Keeper means you keep some players for next year. You draft the rest again.

### BB-141 — What is dynasty?

**Answer:** Dynasty means you keep most or all of your players from year to year.

### BB-142 — Does player value change in dynasty?

**Answer:** Yes. Age and long-term growth matter more because you may keep the player for years.

## Special modes

### BB-143 — What is Lock-In fantasy basketball?

**Answer:** Lock-In is a Sleeper game mode where one real game per player can count for the week.

### BB-144 — How does Lock-In work?

**Answer:** After a player finishes a game, you may lock that game before their next game starts. Once locked, it stays.

### BB-145 — Do all of a player's games count in Lock-In?

**Answer:** No. Only one game per player counts for that week in Sleeper Lock-In.

### BB-146 — Can I undo a locked game in Sleeper Lock-In?

**Answer:** No. Once you lock that game, it stays as that player's game for the week.
