## Scoring formats

### BB-021 — What is a points league?

**Answer:** A points league gives a set point value to each stat. Your player stats add up to one fantasy score.

### BB-022 — What is head-to-head points?

**Answer:** You face one fantasy team for the week. The team with more fantasy points wins the matchup.

### BB-023 — What is a category league?

**Answer:** A category league uses many stat groups. These can include points, boards, assists, steals, and blocks.

### BB-024 — What is head-to-head categories?

**Answer:** You face one team for the week, but each stat group is its own small contest.

### BB-025 — What is a one-win category league?

**Answer:** You still compare stat groups, but the team that wins more groups gets one win for the week.

### BB-026 — What is 9-cat?

**Answer:** Nine-cat is a common format with nine stat groups. The exact nine should be checked in your league.

### BB-027 — What is 8-cat?

**Answer:** Eight-cat uses eight stat groups. It often leaves out turnovers, but your league may use other rules.

### BB-028 — What is roto?

**Answer:** Roto compares your season stats with the whole league. You gain or lose rank in each stat group over time.

### BB-029 — What is season points?

**Answer:** Season points adds your fantasy points across the whole season. You compete with the whole league, not one weekly team.

### BB-030 — Which fantasy basketball format is easiest to learn?

**Answer:** A points league is often easy to learn. All player stats turn into one score.

### BB-031 — Why does scoring format matter so much?

**Answer:** The same player can be worth more in one format than another. Your rules decide which stats help the most.

## Categories

### BB-032 — What does PTS mean?

**Answer:** PTS means points scored by the player in the real game.

### BB-033 — What does REB mean?

**Answer:** REB means rebounds. A rebound happens when a player gets the ball after a missed shot.

### BB-034 — What does AST mean?

**Answer:** AST means assists. An assist is a pass that helps a teammate make a basket.

### BB-035 — What does STL mean?

**Answer:** STL means steals. A steal happens when a player takes the ball from the other team.

### BB-036 — What does BLK mean?

**Answer:** BLK means blocks. A block happens when a player stops or changes a shot with the ball.

### BB-037 — What does 3PM or 3PTM mean?

**Answer:** It means three-point shots made. It counts how many made threes a player has.

### BB-038 — What does FG% mean?

**Answer:** FG% means field goal percent. It shows how often a player makes the shots they take from the floor.

### BB-039 — What does FT% mean?

**Answer:** FT% means free throw percent. It shows how often a player makes free throws.

### BB-040 — What does TO mean?

**Answer:** TO means turnovers. A turnover is when a team loses the ball before getting a shot.

### BB-041 — Is a lower turnover number better?

**Answer:** Yes, in most leagues that count turnovers. Fewer is better for that stat group.

### BB-042 — What does GP mean?

**Answer:** GP means games played. It tells how many games a player or lineup spot has used.

### BB-043 — What is a double-double?

**Answer:** A double-double means a player had 10 or more in two stat groups in one game.

### BB-044 — What is a triple-double?

**Answer:** A triple-double means a player had 10 or more in three stat groups in one game.

### BB-045 — Why can one bad shooting night hurt FG%?

**Answer:** Percent stats use makes and tries. A lot of misses can pull your team percent down.

### BB-046 — Why can one bad free throw shooter hurt FT%?

**Answer:** A player can hurt your team FT% if they take many free throws and miss a lot.

### BB-047 — Can I win a category matchup if I score fewer real points?

**Answer:** Yes. You can lose the points group but win more of the other stat groups.

### BB-048 — What is punting a category?

**Answer:** Punting means you care less about one stat. You try to make other stats stronger.

### BB-049 — Do I have to punt a category?

**Answer:** No. It is one way to build a team, not a rule.

### BB-050 — When should I think about a punt plan?

**Answer:** Look at your best players and league rules first. A plan should fit your team, not a word you heard online.
