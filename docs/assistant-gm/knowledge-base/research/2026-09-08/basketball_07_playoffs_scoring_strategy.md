## Playoffs

### BB-148 — How do fantasy basketball playoffs work?

**Answer:** Teams that qualify play matchups near the end of the fantasy season. The exact weeks and team count come from your league.

### BB-149 — Why does playoff schedule matter?

**Answer:** Some real teams play more games than others in a fantasy playoff week. More games can mean more chances for stats.

### BB-150 — What is a playoff bye?

**Answer:** A playoff bye lets a top fantasy team skip the first playoff round.

### BB-151 — What is a tiebreaker?

**Answer:** A tiebreaker is a rule used when two teams have the same result.

### BB-154 — Do only active players count?

**Answer:** Only players in active spots count in normal lineup play. They must be active when their game starts.

### BB-155 — Why is my bench player showing stats but not helping my score?

**Answer:** You can still see the real stats, but they do not count because the player was on your bench.

### BB-156 — Can a player score fantasy points in more than one real game in a week?

**Answer:** In many daily points leagues, yes. Each game can add to the weekly total. Some game modes work in a different way.

## Strategy

### BB-157 — What should I check each morning in fantasy basketball?

**Answer:** Check who is hurt and who plays today. Check open spots, your add limit, and close stat groups too.

### BB-158 — What should I check at night?

**Answer:** Look at tomorrow's games and injury news. Then see if a short add can fill an open spot.

### BB-159 — What matters more: player skill or schedule?

**Answer:** Both matter. A great player is hard to bench, but schedule can change close calls and short-term adds.

### BB-160 — Why should the GM ask for my scoring format before giving advice?

**Answer:** Because a player can be great in one format and only fair in another. The rules change the answer.
