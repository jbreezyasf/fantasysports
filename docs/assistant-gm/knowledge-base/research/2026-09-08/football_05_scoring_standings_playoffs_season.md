# Assistant GM FAQ — Football
**Status:** Research-backed candidate knowledge. Do not override canonical Big Exec rules or live league state.

## Scoring

### FB-093 — How do fantasy football points work?

**Answer:** Real game stats turn into fantasy points. Your league's scoring rules say how many points each stat is worth.

### FB-094 — What does Half-PPR mean?

**Answer:** Half-PPR means a player gets half a point each time they catch a pass.

### FB-095 — How many points is a touchdown in Big Exec?

**Answer:** A touchdown is worth six points in Big Exec. This is true for a pass, run, catch, or team play.

### FB-096 — How many points is a catch in Big Exec?

**Answer:** A catch is worth half a fantasy point in Big Exec football.

### FB-097 — How many points are passing yards in Big Exec?

**Answer:** A quarterback gets 0.04 points for each passing yard. That is one point for every 25 passing yards.

### FB-098 — How many points are rushing or receiving yards?

**Answer:** A player gets 0.1 points for each rushing or receiving yard. That is one point for every 10 yards.

### FB-099 — What happens when a quarterback throws an interception?

**Answer:** Big Exec takes away two points when a QB throws a pick.

### FB-100 — What happens when a player loses a fumble?

**Answer:** Big Exec takes away two points for a lost fumble.

### FB-101 — How are field goals scored?

**Answer:** Big Exec gives three points for 0 to 39 yards, four for 40 to 49, and five for 50 or more.

### FB-102 — How does D/ST score?

**Answer:** D/ST can score from sacks, picks, blocks, safeties, touchdowns, and points allowed.

### FB-103 — Why did my score change?

**Answer:** A live stat may have changed, or an official stat fix may have come in. I can check the exact score data.

### FB-104 — Is a projection the same as my score?

**Answer:** No. A projection is only a guess. Your score comes from real game stats.

## Standings

### FB-105 — How do standings work?

**Answer:** Standings put teams in order by the league's game results and tie rules.

### FB-106 — What are power rankings?

**Answer:** Power rankings are a fun way to compare teams. They do not decide who makes the Big Exec playoffs.

### FB-107 — What is all-play?

**Answer:** All-play compares your score with every other team that week. It is separate from your main head-to-head result.

### FB-108 — Why did I lose even with a high score?

**Answer:** You only need one team to beat you in head-to-head play: the team you faced that week.

## Playoffs

### FB-109 — How many teams make the Big Exec football playoffs?

**Answer:** Six teams make the main playoffs in the current Big Exec football plan.

### FB-110 — Do the top teams get a playoff bye?

**Answer:** Yes. The top two playoff seeds skip the first playoff round.

### FB-111 — When is the Big Exec football title game?

**Answer:** The current Big Exec plan puts the title game in Week 17.

## Season

### FB-112 — What is The Circuit?

**Answer:** The Circuit is Weeks 1 through 9. Each of the 10 teams plays every other team once.

### FB-113 — What is Rivalry Week?

**Answer:** Rivalry Week is Week 10. Big Exec pairs teams based on league rivalries.

### FB-114 — What is Revenge Week?

**Answer:** Revenge Week is Week 11. It brings back key matchups for another game.

### FB-115 — What is Position Week?

**Answer:** Position Week is Week 12. Teams close to each other in the standings play each other.

### FB-116 — What is Chaos Week?

**Answer:** Chaos Week is Week 13. Top teams face lower teams in a top-versus-bottom setup.

### FB-117 — What is Judgment Week?

**Answer:** Judgment Week is Week 14. It is the last big set of games before the playoffs.

### FB-118 — What if I miss the main playoffs?

**Answer:** Big Exec is built to keep you playing. A second event and team goals can still give you things to win.
