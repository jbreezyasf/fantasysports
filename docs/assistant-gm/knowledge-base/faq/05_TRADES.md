# FAQ — Trades

## FAQ-BE-080 — What is a trade?

**Voice answer:** A trade is an agreement between franchises to exchange roster assets. The deal becomes official only after the required acceptance, validation, and any configured commissioner review.

## FAQ-BE-081 — How do I propose a trade?

**Voice answer:** Open Trade Room, choose the other franchise, select what you are offering and requesting, review both sides, and submit the proposal.

## FAQ-BE-082 — Can I counter a trade?

**Voice answer:** The approved Big Exec trade lifecycle includes counteroffers. The current GM should retrieve whether that action is available for your specific offer.

## FAQ-BE-083 — Can I cancel my offer?

**Voice answer:** The proposer can withdraw or cancel a pending proposal when its current state allows it.

## FAQ-BE-084 — What is the trade deadline?

**Voice answer:** It is the cutoff after which no new trade can be proposed and no pending offer can be accepted. Your exact deadline comes from your league season.

**Tool required:** Retrieve the exact deadline and display timezone.

## FAQ-BE-085 — Can an old offer be accepted after the deadline?

**Voice answer:** No. A proposal created before the deadline cannot be used to bypass the cutoff.

## FAQ-BE-086 — Are waivers still open after trades close?

**Voice answer:** Yes, according to league waiver and free-agency rules.

## FAQ-BE-087 — Is this trade fair?

**Voice answer:** Fairness depends on both rosters, lineup needs, depth, player outlook, and what each manager is trying to accomplish.

**Tool required:** Pro+ Trade Advisor for personalized analysis. Label recommendation versus fact.

## FAQ-BE-088 — Can the Assistant GM accept a trade for me?

**Voice answer:** Not autonomously. Trade analysis may be available before voice trade transactions. Any future supported acceptance still requires the exact deal to be displayed, confirmed, and revalidated.

## FAQ-BE-089 — Can the commissioner reverse or veto a trade?

**Voice answer:** That depends on the configured league rules and current implementation state.

**Tool required:** Retrieve league trade policy; never assume commissioner power.
