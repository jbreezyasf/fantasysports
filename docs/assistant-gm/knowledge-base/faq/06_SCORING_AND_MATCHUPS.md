# FAQ — Scoring and Matchups

## FAQ-BE-100 — How do fantasy points work?

**Voice answer:** Real player statistics are converted into fantasy points using your league's scoring profile. Big Exec's displayed scoring table must match the authoritative scoring engine.

## FAQ-BE-101 — How many points is a touchdown?

**Voice answer:** In Big Exec's approved Pro Football scoring, every supported touchdown is worth six fantasy points, including passing, rushing, receiving, D/ST, and supported return touchdowns.

## FAQ-BE-102 — What does Half-PPR mean?

**Voice answer:** Half-PPR means a player earns half a fantasy point for each reception, in addition to yardage and touchdown points.

## FAQ-BE-103 — Why did my score change?

**Voice answer:** The data provider may have updated a play or issued a stat correction, or a game may still be live. I need the exact matchup and score version to explain your change.

**Tool required:** Current score, raw/source timestamp, correction/audit data.

## FAQ-BE-104 — What is a projection?

**Voice answer:** A projection is an estimate of future fantasy points. It is not your official score and it is not guaranteed.

## FAQ-BE-105 — Am I winning?

**Voice answer:** I need your current matchup score and the players each franchise still has left.

**Tool required:** Current matchup state.

## FAQ-BE-106 — When is a matchup final?

**Voice answer:** A matchup becomes final only after the relevant real games are final and Big Exec completes required scoring and correction processing.

## FAQ-BE-107 — What are Points For?

**Voice answer:** Points For is the total official fantasy score your franchise has produced across counted matchups.

## FAQ-BE-108 — What is all-play?

**Voice answer:** All-play compares your weekly score against all nine other franchises. If you have the third-highest score, your weekly all-play record is seven wins and two losses. It is separate from official head-to-head standings.

## FAQ-BE-109 — Why did I lose even though I scored a lot?

**Voice answer:** Head-to-head fantasy depends on the opponent you faced. You can have one of the week's highest scores and still lose to the only team that scored more. All-play helps show that context.

## FAQ-BE-110 — Do bench points count in my matchup?

**Voice answer:** Not in Big Exec's normal managed weekly lineup. Only legal starters count.
