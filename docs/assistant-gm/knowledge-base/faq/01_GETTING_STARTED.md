# FAQ — Getting Started

## FAQ-BE-001 — What is fantasy football?

**Voice answer:** Fantasy football lets you draft real football players onto your own franchise. Their real-game statistics become fantasy points, and your weekly lineup competes against another manager's lineup.

**Follow-up:** Want me to explain the draft or show you what you need to do first?

## FAQ-BE-002 — What is Big Exec?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec is a fantasy sports platform built around running a franchise, not just filling out a lineup. You draft, trade, use waivers, compete, build rivalries, earn achievements, and preserve your franchise history.

## FAQ-BE-003 — What do I need to do every week?

**Voice answer:** Check your starting lineup, replace players who are out or on a bye, review waiver options, respond to trades, and make sure every starter is set before that player's real game begins.

**Tool note:** Offer to inspect the manager's current lineup.

## FAQ-BE-004 — Do I need to know football already?

**Voice answer:** No. The standard Assistant GM can explain the game and Big Exec controls. In an Executive league, Assistant GM Pro+ can also review your full franchise and recommend what to do next.

## FAQ-BE-005 — What is a franchise?

**Voice answer:** Your franchise is your persistent Big Exec team identity. Its name, manager, history, championships, rivalries, awards, and Stadium can continue even when the player roster changes next season.

## FAQ-BE-006 — How many managers are in a Big Exec football league?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec's initial Pro Football format is built for 10 franchises.

## FAQ-BE-007 — What happens after I join a league?

**Voice answer:** You create your franchise identity, prepare for the draft, draft your roster, set your lineup, and then begin weekly competition.

## FAQ-BE-008 — Where should I go first?

**Voice answer:** Start in your Front Office. It shows your franchise, league, record, urgent actions, and the next place you need to go.

## FAQ-BE-009 — What is the Front Office?

**Voice answer:** The Front Office is your command center. It points you to the Draft Room or Free Agency, Locker Room, Trade Room, League News, and anything that needs your attention.

## FAQ-BE-010 — Is Big Exec free?

**Voice answer:** Core fantasy gameplay and accessibility are available without an Executive purchase. A commissioner can buy the Executive League Season Pass to give every manager in that league Assistant GM Pro+ and premium league intelligence.
