# FAQ — Drafting

## FAQ-BE-020 — What is a fantasy draft?

**Voice answer:** The draft is where every manager takes turns selecting real players for their fantasy roster. Once a player is drafted in your league, another franchise cannot draft that same player.

## FAQ-BE-021 — What is a snake draft?

**Voice answer:** In a snake draft, the order reverses every round. If managers pick one through ten in Round 1, they pick ten through one in Round 2.

## FAQ-BE-022 — How long do I have to make a pick?

**Voice answer:** That depends on your league's draft timer.

**Tool required:** Retrieve the current draft/league setting.

## FAQ-BE-023 — What happens if my clock expires?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec checks your personal queue first. If no legal queued player is available, it uses the platform ranking and roster rules to make an automatic pick.

## FAQ-BE-024 — What is my draft queue?

**Voice answer:** Your draft queue is your private ordered list of players you want. If your clock expires, Big Exec tries to draft the highest legal player still available from that list.

## FAQ-BE-025 — What positions should I draft?

**Voice answer:** You need enough players to fill every required starting position plus your bench. Draft value matters, but ignoring a required position can leave you with an incomplete lineup.

**Tool note:** Pro+ should inspect roster construction and remaining players.

## FAQ-BE-026 — What does best available mean?

**Voice answer:** Best available means the highest-valued undrafted player according to the current ranking. It does not always mean that player is the best choice for your roster.

## FAQ-BE-027 — What is a reach?

**Voice answer:** A reach is selecting a player noticeably earlier than their expected draft value. A reach can still make sense when you need the position or believe the player will not return to you.

## FAQ-BE-028 — What is positional scarcity?

**Voice answer:** Positional scarcity means useful players are running out faster at one position than another. It can make taking the last strong tight end more valuable than taking another receiver from a deep group.

## FAQ-BE-029 — Can two teams draft the same player?

**Voice answer:** No. Big Exec uses league-wide ownership and authoritative draft validation to prevent duplicate picks.

## FAQ-BE-030 — Can the commissioner pause or correct the draft?

**Voice answer:** Supported commissioner controls include pausing or resuming the draft and correcting or undoing the latest pick with an audit trail.

## FAQ-BE-031 — Can the Assistant GM draft for me?

**Voice answer:** The GM can recommend or prepare a pick. In Beta, it cannot complete your intentional voice-requested pick without confirming the exact player and rechecking that the player is still available and that you are on the clock.

## FAQ-BE-032 — Who should I draft?

**Voice answer:** I need your league scoring, current roster, draft position, players already selected, and players still available before I can answer that responsibly.

**Tool required:** Draft state and available-player tools. Pro+ returns full strategic recommendation; Standard may return basic current information.
