# FAQ — Season and Playoffs

## FAQ-BE-120 — How do official standings work?

**Voice answer:** Official standings use your league's head-to-head results and approved tiebreakers. They determine playoff qualification; power rankings and all-play do not replace them.

## FAQ-BE-121 — What are power rankings?

**Voice answer:** Power rankings are an analysis and entertainment view built from deterministic inputs. They do not determine official playoff positions.

## FAQ-BE-122 — What is The Circuit?

**Voice answer:** The Circuit is Big Exec's Weeks 1 through 9 round-robin phase, where 10 franchises play each opponent once.

## FAQ-BE-123 — What is Rivalry Week?

**Voice answer:** Rivalry Week is Week 10, when Big Exec pairs franchises using their rivalry relationships.

## FAQ-BE-124 — What is Revenge Week?

**Voice answer:** Revenge Week is Week 11 and creates strategic rematches.

## FAQ-BE-125 — What is Position Week?

**Voice answer:** Position Week is Week 12. Teams are paired by standing: first plays second, third plays fourth, and so on.

## FAQ-BE-126 — What is Chaos Week?

**Voice answer:** Chaos Week is Week 13. The top franchise plays tenth, second plays ninth, and the rest follow that top-versus-bottom pattern.

## FAQ-BE-127 — What is Judgment Week?

**Voice answer:** Judgment Week is Week 14 and creates high-stakes matchups immediately before the postseason.

## FAQ-BE-128 — How many teams make the playoffs?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec's approved initial format sends six franchises to the championship playoffs, with the top two seeds receiving first-round byes.

## FAQ-BE-129 — What happens if I miss the championship playoffs?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec is designed to keep all 10 managers competing through Week 17. A separate postseason and franchise achievements give eliminated managers something meaningful to win.

## FAQ-BE-130 — Does the Assistant GM decide who makes the playoffs?

**Voice answer:** No. Deterministic Big Exec rules calculate qualification and seeding. The GM can only explain those results.
