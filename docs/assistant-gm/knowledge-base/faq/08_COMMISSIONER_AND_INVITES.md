# FAQ — Commissioner and Invitations

## FAQ-BE-140 — What does the commissioner do?

**Voice answer:** The commissioner creates and manages the league, invites managers, schedules and oversees the draft, and controls only the settings and corrective actions Big Exec authorizes.

## FAQ-BE-141 — How do I invite someone?

**Voice answer:** Open the league invitation controls, enter the person's exact email address, review it, and send the invitation.

**Tool note:** Voice flow must prepare, read, phonetically spell, confirm, and only then send.

## FAQ-BE-142 — Can I invite several people by voice?

**Voice answer:** Yes. Each address must remain individually reviewable, and every address must be phonetically verified before the first send confirmation.

## FAQ-BE-143 — Why does the GM spell email addresses?

**Voice answer:** Email addresses can sound alike while being spelled differently. Phonetic readback helps catch the mistake before an invitation goes to the wrong address.

## FAQ-BE-144 — Can the GM guess someone's email from their name?

**Voice answer:** No. It will not guess, search private contacts automatically, or choose among possible addresses without your direction.

## FAQ-BE-145 — What happens if one invitation fails?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec reports success or failure for each recipient. A partial failure must not be described as though every invitation was sent.

## FAQ-BE-146 — How many franchises can join?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec V1 Pro Football is built for 10 franchises. The system must prevent invitations or joins that exceed league capacity.

## FAQ-BE-147 — Who buys the Executive League Season Pass?

**Voice answer:** The commissioner purchases the pass for a specific league, sport, and season. Every authorized manager in that league receives Assistant GM Pro+.

## FAQ-BE-148 — Can a commissioner reopen a passed trade deadline?

**Voice answer:** No. Big Exec's current authoritative rule prevents a deadline that has already passed from being reopened.

## FAQ-BE-149 — Can a commissioner change official scores however they want?

**Voice answer:** Commissioner powers must follow Big Exec's authorized correction and audit rules. The Assistant GM cannot invent or silently alter official scoring.
