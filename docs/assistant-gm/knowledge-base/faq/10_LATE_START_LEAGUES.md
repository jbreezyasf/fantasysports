# FAQ — Late-Start Leagues

## FAQ-BE-180 — Can I create a football league after the season starts?

**Voice answer:** Yes. Big Exec can accept new Pro Football leagues through the applicable league-creation cutoff, which is no later than the season's trade deadline.

**Tool required:** Retrieve current sport/season cutoff state.

## FAQ-BE-181 — Can I join after the trade deadline?

**Voice answer:** You can still create a Big Exec account and join sports whose enrollment is open, but you cannot create or join a new Pro Football league-season after its cutoff.

## FAQ-BE-182 — Do late-start leagues still draft?

**Voice answer:** Yes. All 10 franchises complete a normal draft and create their franchise identities before backfill begins.

## FAQ-BE-183 — What happens to the weeks we missed?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec uses the players selected in your completed draft, creates a legal simulated lineup for each completed missed week, calculates official fantasy points from that week's historical stats, runs the scheduled matchups, and updates standings before live play begins.

## FAQ-BE-184 — How is my simulated lineup chosen?

**Voice answer:** For each missed completed week, Big Exec selects the highest-scoring legal lineup from the roster produced by your draft. Every franchise uses the same deterministic rule.

## FAQ-BE-185 — Can I change a missed week's lineup?

**Voice answer:** No. Backfilled weeks are generated under one league-wide rule and locked after validation. Later roster moves do not rewrite them.

## FAQ-BE-186 — Do post-draft trades or waivers affect missed weeks?

**Voice answer:** No. Only the roster created by the late draft is eligible for retroactive scoring. Later transactions apply going forward.

## FAQ-BE-187 — Does the current week get simulated?

**Voice answer:** No completed backfill is created for an in-progress week. The league becomes live at the next safe weekly boundary defined by the sport calendar.

**Tool required:** Retrieve current week and activation boundary.

## FAQ-BE-188 — Are backfilled scores official?

**Voice answer:** Yes, after the league-wide backfill finishes, validation passes, and the results are finalized. They affect standings, all-play, and applicable season records.

## FAQ-BE-189 — Will the GM explain how my record was created?

**Voice answer:** Yes. It can explain which weeks were backfilled, which drafted roster was used, each simulated lineup, matchup result, and the rule applied.

## FAQ-BE-190 — Can a late-start league buy Executive?

**Voice answer:** Yes. The commissioner can purchase the Executive League Season Pass for that league, sport, and season while purchase eligibility is open.

## FAQ-BE-191 — Can another sport still be open after football closes?

**Voice answer:** Yes. Enrollment is controlled separately for each sport and season. Closing Pro Football does not automatically close basketball, baseball, soccer, or future supported sports.

## FAQ-BE-192 — Why is there a cutoff?

**Voice answer:** Late entry must still leave enough real competition before trades close and the postseason begins. The cutoff protects league integrity and prevents a league from being created after its meaningful management window has ended.
