# FAQ — Waivers and Free Agency

## FAQ-BE-060 — What is free agency?

**Voice answer:** Free agency is the pool of unrostered players who can be added immediately when league rules allow it.

## FAQ-BE-061 — What are waivers?

**Voice answer:** Waivers temporarily hold a player instead of letting the fastest manager grab them. Managers submit claims, and the league's waiver rules decide who receives the player.

## FAQ-BE-062 — What is a waiver claim?

**Voice answer:** A waiver claim is your request to add a player when waivers process. If your roster is full, the claim also identifies the player you will drop if you win.

## FAQ-BE-063 — How is waiver priority decided?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec's initial priority favors franchises lower in the official standings when waivers process. Points For and a documented final tiebreaker resolve ties. Before standings are meaningful, the league can use its approved preseason fallback.

## FAQ-BE-064 — Why didn't I win my waiver claim?

**Voice answer:** Another valid claim may have had higher priority, your claim may have become invalid, or your roster/drop selection may not have satisfied league rules.

**Tool required:** Retrieve exact claim and processing result.

## FAQ-BE-065 — What happens to the player I drop?

**Voice answer:** When league rules require it, the dropped player enters a waiver hold so every eligible franchise has a fair chance to claim them.

## FAQ-BE-066 — Can I cancel a waiver claim?

**Voice answer:** You can withdraw a pending claim before it processes when the current league state allows it.

**Tool required:** Retrieve pending claim and deadline.

## FAQ-BE-067 — Do waivers stop at the trade deadline?

**Voice answer:** No. Big Exec waivers and free-agent acquisition continue after trading closes according to league rules.

## FAQ-BE-068 — Why can't I drop a player?

**Voice answer:** The player may be locked, protected by roster-integrity rules, involved in a pending transaction, or the move may make your roster illegal.

**Tool required:** Retrieve exact roster and rule state.

## FAQ-BE-069 — Who should I claim?

**Voice answer:** I need your roster needs, the actual available players, waiver position or budget, injuries, upcoming byes, and who you would have to drop.

## FAQ-BE-070 — Can the GM submit a claim for me?

**Voice answer:** It can prepare the exact add, drop, and budget or priority context. You must confirm, and Big Exec must revalidate everything before submission.
