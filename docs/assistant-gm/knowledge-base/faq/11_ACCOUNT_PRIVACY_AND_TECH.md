# FAQ — Account, Privacy, and Technical Help

## FAQ-BE-200 — Why isn't my microphone working?

**Voice answer:** Check that microphone permission is allowed for Big Exec, confirm another app is not using it, and try Ask GM again. You can always type instead.

## FAQ-BE-201 — Is Big Exec always listening?

**Voice answer:** No. Beta uses push-to-talk. Recording begins only after your action and stops when the bounded request ends or you cancel it.

## FAQ-BE-202 — Is my raw voice recording stored?

**Voice answer:** Big Exec's specified default is not to retain raw voice audio. Transcript retention follows the approved conversation and privacy policy.

**Policy note:** Retrieve current deployed privacy policy before making a legally binding claim.

## FAQ-BE-203 — Why did the GM hear the wrong player?

**Voice answer:** Player names can sound alike or speech recognition can mishear them. Big Exec should ask for clarification and show the exact player before preparing an action.

## FAQ-BE-204 — What if spoken audio fails?

**Voice answer:** The full text answer remains available. You can replay, use your screen reader, or continue by typing.

## FAQ-BE-205 — Why does the GM say it cannot retrieve something?

**Voice answer:** The required Big Exec data or tool may be unavailable. The GM is designed to admit that limitation instead of inventing a score, roster, player status, or deadline.

## FAQ-BE-206 — Why am I in Standard mode?

**Voice answer:** Pro+ applies only inside a league-season with an active Executive entitlement. You may be viewing a different Free league, or the entitlement may be pending, expired, refunded, revoked, or unavailable.

**Tool required:** Retrieve current entitlement state.

## FAQ-BE-207 — Can I delete a conversation?

**Voice answer:** Conversation deletion must follow the current retention controls available in the product.

**Tool/policy required:** Do not promise a control until it is implemented.

## FAQ-BE-208 — How do I report a wrong answer?

**Voice answer:** Use the feedback or support control attached to the GM response and include what you expected. Big Exec should preserve enough request and tool evidence to investigate without requiring unnecessary raw audio.
