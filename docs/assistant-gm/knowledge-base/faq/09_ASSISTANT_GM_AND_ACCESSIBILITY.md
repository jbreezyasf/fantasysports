# FAQ — Assistant GM, Voice, Accessibility, and Executive

## FAQ-BE-160 — What is the Assistant GM?

**Voice answer:** The Assistant GM helps you understand Big Exec, retrieve authorized league information, and make fantasy decisions. It can explain and recommend, but Big Exec's deterministic systems remain authoritative.

## FAQ-BE-161 — Can I talk instead of typing?

**Voice answer:** Yes. Use Ask GM to speak, review the transcript, and hear the response. You can also type, stop the audio, or replay the last answer.

## FAQ-BE-162 — Is voice a paid feature?

**Voice answer:** Accessibility voice is not premium. Speech input, spoken output needed for access, transcripts, and supported accessible core tasks remain available without an Executive purchase.

## FAQ-BE-163 — What is Assistant GM Pro+?

**Voice answer:** Pro+ adds deeper and proactive franchise intelligence, including the Executive Draft War Room, full-lineup review, waiver strategy, trade analysis, Front Office Briefs, opponent scouting, season planning, and advanced scenarios.

## FAQ-BE-164 — How do I get Pro+?

**Voice answer:** The commissioner purchases the Big Exec Executive League Season Pass for that league, sport, and season. Pro+ then becomes available to every authorized manager in that league.

## FAQ-BE-165 — Is Pro+ a separate purchase?

**Voice answer:** No. It is included with the $99 Executive League Season Pass and is not sold separately at launch.

## FAQ-BE-166 — Does Pro+ make the league pay-to-win?

**Voice answer:** The pass upgrades the entire league, so every manager receives Pro+. Official rules and outcomes remain the same for everyone.

## FAQ-BE-167 — Can the GM make mistakes?

**Voice answer:** Yes, which is why league facts must come from authorized Big Exec tools and consequential actions require exact confirmation. If data is unavailable, the GM should say so instead of guessing.

## FAQ-BE-168 — Can I turn off the GM voice?

**Voice answer:** Yes. You can use text, stop spoken playback, and use your device's accessibility tools instead.

## FAQ-BE-169 — Does the GM act on its own?

**Voice answer:** Not in Beta. It can proactively alert or recommend, but it cannot silently draft, change a lineup, submit a waiver, drop a player, accept a trade, or send an invitation.

## FAQ-BE-170 — Can the GM read my other leagues?

**Voice answer:** It only receives authorized context for the league you are currently using. Pro+ status is checked separately for each league, sport, and season.
