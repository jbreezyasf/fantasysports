# FAQ — Rosters and Lineups

## FAQ-BE-040 — What is a roster?

**Voice answer:** Your roster is every player your franchise currently owns, including starters, bench players, and eligible injured-reserve players.

## FAQ-BE-041 — What is a starting lineup?

**Voice answer:** Your starting lineup is the group whose fantasy points count in your weekly matchup. Bench-player points normally do not count.

## FAQ-BE-042 — What is FLEX?

**Voice answer:** FLEX is a flexible starting position. In Big Exec Pro Football, it can be filled by an eligible running back, wide receiver, or tight end.

## FAQ-BE-043 — What is the bench?

**Voice answer:** Your bench holds rostered players who are not currently starting. Their points do not count unless the scoring mode explicitly says otherwise.

## FAQ-BE-044 — What is IR?

**Voice answer:** IR is an injured-reserve roster slot for a player whose approved injury status makes them eligible. Eligibility depends on current player designation and league rules.

## FAQ-BE-045 — What does questionable mean?

**Voice answer:** Questionable means the player's availability is uncertain. Check again close to kickoff because the final active or inactive decision may come later.

## FAQ-BE-046 — What is a bye week?

**Voice answer:** A bye week is a scheduled week when a real team does not play. A player on bye normally scores zero, so you should replace them in your starting lineup.

## FAQ-BE-047 — When does my player lock?

**Voice answer:** Each player locks when that player's real game begins. Big Exec does not lock your entire weekly roster just because the first game of the week started.

## FAQ-BE-048 — Why can't I move this player?

**Voice answer:** The player may already be locked, the move may violate position eligibility, or it may create an illegal lineup.

**Tool required:** Retrieve exact lineup, lock, and legality state.

## FAQ-BE-049 — Do bench points count?

**Voice answer:** Not in a normal managed lineup. Bench production remains visible but does not count toward the matchup score.

## FAQ-BE-050 — Who should I start?

**Voice answer:** I need to compare your eligible players, injury status, kickoff times, matchup context, and current projections.

**Tool required:** Current roster/lineup/player data. Full-lineup optimization is Pro+.

## FAQ-BE-051 — Can the GM change my lineup automatically?

**Voice answer:** No. It can recommend and prepare a move, but you must confirm the exact change. Big Exec rechecks the roster and lock state before committing it.
