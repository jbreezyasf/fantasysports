# FAQ Source and Review Register

## Purpose

External platforms were reviewed to identify recurring question families, not to copy their product rules or wording. Big Exec answers come from Big Exec's canonical specifications and current authorized state.

## Primary external sources reviewed

- Sleeper Fantasy Football Support collection: league setup, drafting, waivers/FAAB, trading, scoring, commissioner controls, lineups, IR, playoffs, league history, and technical FAQs.  
  https://support.sleeper.com/en/collections/410900-fantasy-football
- NFL Fantasy — Creating & Reactivating Leagues: custom-league creation and late-start retroactive scoring precedent.  
  https://support.nfl.com/hc/en-us/articles/35869722236436-Creating-Reactivating-Leagues
- Sleeper — How to Make a Midseason Switch: retroactive scores/records and recalculation precedent.  
  https://support.sleeper.com/en/articles/9734528-how-to-make-a-midseason-switch-to-sleeper
- Yahoo Fantasy Football help search/results reviewed for common categories including default settings, waivers, lineup deadlines, scoring, and trades. Yahoo pages returned rate limits during full-page retrieval, so no Big Exec rule relies solely on those pages.

## Review cadence

- Before each sport-season launch.
- After any scoring, roster, waiver, trade, schedule, playoff, payment, accessibility, or entitlement rule change.
- After a repeated unsupported question appears in production analytics.
- After a material correction or user-reported wrong answer.

## Prohibited use

- Do not present another platform's rule as Big Exec's rule.
- Do not scrape or reproduce copyrighted help-center articles.
- Do not use this register instead of current Big Exec structured state.
